  - OLTP vs OLAP vs HTAP — and if it claims HTAP, how it physically separates   
  the two workloads (separate replicas, columnar secondary index, etc.).        
                                                                                
  Where it sits on CAP under partition: CP (refuses writes to stay consistent)  
  or AP (stays up, reconciles later)? CAP is a coarse lens, though — PACELC is  
  more useful because it also tells you the latency-vs-consistency tradeoff     
  when there's no partition.                                                    
  Default isolation level and what's actually achievable (read committed,       
  snapshot, serializable). Many "ACID" claims mean snapshot isolation, not      
  serializable.                                                                 
  Replication model: single-leader, multi-leader, leaderless quorum (R+W>N)?    
  Synchronous vs async, and what the failover/split-brain story is.             
  Tunable consistency? (Cassandra/Dynamo-style per-query consistency levels.)   
  Clock dependency — does correctness rest on synchronized clocks               
  (Spanner/TrueTime, hybrid logical clocks)?                                    
                                                                                
  Schema                                                                        
                                                                                
  Schema-on-write vs schema-on-read; rigid, flexible, or schemaless.            
  "Schemaless" usually just means the schema lives in your application code     
  instead of the DB.                                                            
  Migration/evolution story — online DDL, or does an ALTER lock the table?      
  Type system richness (native JSON, arrays, geospatial, vectors, intervals).   
                                                                                
  Query interface                                                               
                                                                                
  Query language: SQL (which dialect / standard compliance), a custom DSL (CQL, 
  AQL, Cypher/GQL, PromQL), or API-only (get/put)?                              
  Transaction support: full multi-statement ACID, single-row atomicity only, or 
  none?                                                                         
  Secondary indexes, joins, aggregations, window functions — what's native vs   
  what you reimplement in the app.                                              
  Stored procedures / UDFs, and in what language.                               
                                                                                
  Scaling & topology                                                            
                                                                                
  Vertical vs horizontal scaling; sharding (auto vs manual, resharding pain),   
  partitioning scheme.                                                          
  Read replicas, and whether reads from them are consistent.                    
  Separation of storage and compute (the Snowflake/Aurora/Neon pattern) —       
  matters a lot for elasticity and cost.                                        
                                                                                
  Performance & durability                                                      
                                                                                
  Write path durability: WAL, fsync policy, group commit. What's the data-loss  
  window on crash?                                                              
  Read/write throughput and latency profile under your access pattern; tail     
  latencies, not just averages.                                                 
  Compaction / vacuum / GC behavior and its impact on p99.                      
                                                                                
  Operations                                                                    
                                                                                
  Backup/restore, point-in-time recovery, snapshotting.                         
  Observability: metrics, query plans/EXPLAIN, slow-query logs.                 
  Upgrade story (rolling, downtime), and the day-2 operational burden generally 
  — this is where most engines hide their real cost.                            
  Maturity: production track record, known failure modes, the Aphyr/Jepsen      
  result if one exists.                                                         
                                                                                
  Ecosystem & people                                                            
                                                                                
  Typical users / canonical use cases — and just as informative, the            
  anti-patterns where it's the wrong tool.                                      
  Client drivers, ORM support, connectors (CDC, Kafka, dbt, BI tools).          
  Community size, commercial support, documentation quality.                    
                                                                                
  Licensing & cost                                                              
                                                                                
  OSS license and which flavor — permissive (Apache/MIT/BSD) vs copyleft vs a   
  source-available "open-ish" license (SSPL, BSL/Elastic, Confluent Community). 
  The post-2018 relicensing wave (Mongo, Elastic, Redis, Cockroach, HashiCorp)  
  matters if you're embedding or reselling.                                     
  Self-managed vs managed-only; vendor lock-in via proprietary extensions.      
  Cost model: per-node, per-core, per-GB stored, per-query/serverless — and how 
  it behaves at your scale, since the cheap-at-small ones often invert at       
  scale.                                                                        
                                                                                
  Hardware / deployment                                                         
                                                                                
  Memory-bound vs disk-bound vs CPU-bound; does it want to fit the working set  
  (or all) of data in RAM?                                                      
  Storage assumptions — NVMe vs spinning disk, local vs network-attached (some  
  engines tolerate EBS-style latency, some really don't).                       
  Single-node, clustered, embedded (SQLite/DuckDB/RocksDB style), or            
  serverless.                                                                   
  Cloud-native vs on-prem; container/k8s friendliness, stateful-set realities.  
                                                                                
  - SaaS/on-prem                                                                
  - learning curve/complexity/size of typical team                              
  - does it have a big ecosystem and community and engineer availability
